Step 10 - Front/rear correction and photo angle
===============================================

If one end of the car clearly dominates (grille, headlights, bonnet, windshield vs tail lights, boot, rear glass by 3x), a bumper labelled for the other end is relabelled. Then the photo angle is inferred from where the front and rear parts sit in the image, which gives left/right for each damaged panel.
