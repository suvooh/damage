Step 11 - Damage on panels and intensity rating
===============================================

Each damage region takes the part under most of its pixels. Damage area is measured as a % of that panel for each damage type, then rated 1-5 by the rubric (dent depth x area; scratch < 20% = 1 else 3; paint like scratch; crack/deformation on the d3 row). The panel takes its worst rating.

 front_bumper: panel rating 3 (moderate), damage covers 3.4% of the panel
    - crack_or_breakage: 2.4% of panel -> 3  (crack or breakage, 2% of panel (not in the rubric; rated on the d3 row))
    - deformation_or_detachment: 0.9% of panel -> 3  (deformation or detachment, 1% of panel (not in the rubric; rated on the d3 row))
