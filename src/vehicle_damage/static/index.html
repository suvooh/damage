How the intensity is rated

Each damage is rated 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest) from its type and how much of its panel it covers.

Scratches: under 20% of the panel = 1, otherwise 3. Each damage type on a panel is rated on its own total area, and the panel shows its worst rating. Not in the supplied rubric: paint damage is rated like a scratch, and cracks, breakage and deformation are rated on the d3 row by panel area. Dent depth is not measured yet, so dents are assumed d2 and marked "assumed". The model often confuses scratches, paint damage and cracks, so check the type before trusting a high rating.

Development model, not production approved. Results are a triage aid, not a repair quote. Each photo takes a few seconds on the GPU and about a minute on CPU.
